Custom Shapes

Select one or more shapes on the canvas and choose Objects > Save as New Shape... to add them to your own shape library. The dialog asks for:

  • Category – an existing custom category, or New Category... to create one.
  • Name – how the shape appears in the toolbox (also its tooltip).

Saved shapes show up as new categories in the toolbox's category dropdown, alongside Basic/Flowchart/UML/Assorted, each with its own grid of shape buttons – click one and click-and-drag on the canvas exactly like a built-in shape.

Each category can hold up to 50 shapes; once a category is full, save further shapes under a new category instead. Custom shapes are stored per-user (not inside any particular .pydia file), so they're available in every diagram you open.
