Connecting Shapes With Lines

Every shape has a handful of built-in connection points, shown as small “⨉” marks when View > Show Connection Points is on. Draw a Line (or an Arc, Polyline, Zigzagline or Bezierline) starting or ending on one of these marks, and the line attaches itself to that shape: move or resize the shape afterwards, and the line's end follows it automatically, keeping the connection intact.

Adding your own connection points

If a shape's built-in connection points aren't where you need one, select the shape, switch to the Anchor tool in the toolbox, and click on the shape where you want the new point. Lines can attach to it exactly the same way as to a built-in point.

Line styles and arrows

A line's own Properties... dialog (Ctrl+click it, or select it and press Alt+Return) offers:

  • Color – solid, gradient or pattern, same as any other shape's color (see Colors, gradients and patterns).
  • Width and Style – Solid, Dashed, Dash-Dot, Dash-Dot-Dot, or Dotted.
  • Start arrow and End arrow, independently – None, Line Arrow, Filled/Hollow Triangle, Filled/Hollow Diamond, Filled/Hollow Circle, Filled/Hollow Square, or Half Head.

The toolbox's default-properties panel (see The toolbox and drawing shapes) sets these same options for lines you're about to draw.
