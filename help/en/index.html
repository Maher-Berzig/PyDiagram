PyDiagram Help

PyDiagram is a diagram editor for drawing flowcharts, UML diagrams and general-purpose 2D diagrams: place shapes on a canvas, connect them with lines, organize them into layers, and export the result.

The basics

  • Toolbox – the panel on the left holds every shape, grouped into tabs: Basic, Flowchart, UML and Assorted, plus any custom categories of your own. Click a shape's button, then click-and-drag on the canvas to draw it.
  • Moving and resizing – click the Select tool (or press Space to jump back to it) and drag a shape to move it, or drag one of its corner handles to resize it.
  • Connecting shapes – draw a Line starting or ending on another shape's small “⨉” connection points; the line then follows that shape wherever it moves. Use the Anchor tool to add extra connection points of your own to a selected shape.
  • Colors – every fill/border color button can be a plain solid color, a two-color gradient (linear or radial), or a repeating pattern – see Colors, gradients and patterns.
  • Rotate / Flip – in the Objects menu, or on the toolbar, for turning a shape without needing a separate mirrored copy of it. Ctrl+R / Ctrl+Shift+R rotate the selection by +1° / −1°, and Ctrl+Shift+H / Ctrl+Shift+V flip it horizontally / vertically.
  • Properties – Ctrl+click a shape (or select it and press Alt+Return) to change its colors, line style and any size parameters it has.
  • Layers – the Layers panel controls which layer new shapes are added to, and can show, hide, lock or reorder layers.
  • PDF links – a shape can link to another diagram in an exported PDF – see PDF links.
  • Undo / Redo – Ctrl+Z / Ctrl+Shift+Z, for every action above.
