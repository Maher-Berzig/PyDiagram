Layers

The Layers panel (F8 to show/hide it) lists every layer in the current diagram, top of the stack first.

  • Checkbox – toggles that layer's visibility on the canvas, without changing which layer is current or selecting the row.
  • Clicking a row – makes that layer the current one: newly drawn shapes are added to it, and only its own shapes can be selected on the canvas.
  • Double-clicking a row's name – renames that layer.
  • New – adds a new layer, asking for its name.
  • Up / Down – moves the selected layer higher or lower in the stacking order.
  • Del – deletes the selected layer (and everything on it). The last remaining layer can't be deleted.

Each open diagram has its own independent set of layers, just as it has its own undo history (see Opening a diagram).
