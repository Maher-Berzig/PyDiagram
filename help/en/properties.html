Shape Properties

Ctrl+click any shape (or select it and press Alt+Return) to open its Properties dialog. Text is the one exception: since double-click already enters its own inline editing mode, its Properties dialog is instead reached by right-clicking it and choosing Properties. What's in it depends on the kind of shape, but most share a common core:

  • Fill color and/or Border color – solid, gradient or pattern; see Colors, gradients and patterns.
  • Border width and Border style – Solid, Dashed, Dash-Dot, Dash-Dot-Dot, or Dotted.
  • Draw background – some shapes can be drawn with no fill at all (outline only) by unchecking this.
  • PDF Link – at the bottom of every shape's dialog (Text included): pick another open diagram to make the shape a clickable link to it in exported PDFs. See PDF links.

Shape-specific fields

  • Box (Rectangle) – corner rounding, in addition to the fields above.
  • Line, Polyline, Zigzagline, Bezierline, Arc – a line color, width and style, plus independent Start arrow and End arrow styles – see Connecting shapes with lines.
  • Regular Polygon / Star – number of sides/points and corner rounding (and, for Star, an inner and outer corner rounding).
  • Three-Quarter Circle and Spiral – their own shape parameters alongside the common fill/border fields.
  • Class (UML) – class name, an Attributes list and an Operations list (one per line), and a fill color.
  • Image – the image file path, a Browse... button to pick one, and a Create from LaTeX... button; see Inserting LaTeX equations.
  • Text – the text itself, font family and weight (Normal/Bold/Italic/Bold-Italic), size, alignment (Left/Center/Right), text color, fill color, and whether to draw a background behind the text.
  • Plot – see below.

The Plot shape

Plot draws the graph of up to two functions of x, with its Properties dialog split across three tabs:

  • Functions & Range – the expressions f1(x) and f2(x), each with its own line color; the X and Y axis ranges; an X range (Fill x1 / Fill x2) to shade the area under a curve; and the number of sample points used to draw it.
  • Axes & Ticks – the axis style, whether to show a background grid, whether to show X/Y tick labels, and the interval between ticks (or Auto).
  • Lines & Fill – the curve line width; an x1/x2 boundary lines group (their colors, width, style, dash length, and whether to show them at all); and a Curves and fill group for the color filling the area between the curves and the axis/border color.
