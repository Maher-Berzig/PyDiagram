Zooming and Panels

Zoom

  • Zoom In (Ctrl++) / Zoom Out (Ctrl+-)
  • Normal Size (Ctrl+1) – resets to 100%.
  • Zoom submenu – jumps straight to a preset level: 800%, 400%, 200%, 100%, 50%, 25%, or 12%.
  • Best Fit (Ctrl+F) – zooms and scrolls so the whole diagram fits in the window.
  • The toolbox's Zoom tool mode also lets you scroll the mouse wheel over the canvas to zoom.

Panels

Every dockable panel can be shown or hidden from the View menu, in addition to right-clicking the toolbar/dock area itself:

  • Shapes (F7) – the toolbox.
  • Layers (F8)
  • Tools (F9) – the icon toolbar across the top.
  • Diagram Tree (F10)

The rulers along the canvas edges have their own toggle, Show Rulers (F6), just above these in the View menu.

Other

  • Refresh (F5) – forces the canvas to redraw from scratch; occasionally useful if a stale bit of drawing is ever left behind.
  • Language (View > Language) – switches the interface (menus, dialogs and this Help) between English, Français and العربية. The toolbar's language button offers the same choice.
