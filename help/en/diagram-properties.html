Grid, Snapping and Diagram Properties

Diagram > Properties... opens a dialog with three tabs:

Paper Sizes tab

  • Paper size – Best Fit (the canvas simply grows to fit whatever is drawn, with no fixed page boundary), any of the standard ISO A0–A10 or B0–B10 sizes (each listed with its width × height in millimeters), or Custom Size.
  • Paper orientation – portrait or landscape; landscape swaps the selected size's width and height.
  • Custom width / Custom height – the page's dimensions in millimeters; only enabled while Paper size is set to Custom Size.

Whichever paper size is chosen is drawn on the canvas as a page boundary: shapes outside it stay fully editable, but page-based exports (PNG, SVG, PDF) clip to it. Under Best Fit no boundary is drawn, and exports instead use a rectangle that follows the diagram's own content, with a small margin. The chosen paper size, orientation and custom dimensions also become the default for every new diagram tab afterwards.

Grid tab

  • Visible – shows or hides the grid lines.
  • Snap to – snaps shapes to the grid while drawing or moving them.
  • X Size / Y Size – independent horizontal and vertical grid spacing.
  • Color – the grid lines' color.

Colors tab

  • Background – the diagram canvas's background color.

Quick toggles in the View menu

The same Grid visibility and Snap to Grid settings, plus two more, are also available as quick checkboxes in the View menu, for changing them without opening the dialog:

  • Show Grid / Snap To Grid
  • Snap To Objects – snaps to other shapes' edges and connection points as you draw or move things, in addition to (or instead of) the grid.
  • Show Connection Points – shows or hides every shape's “⨉” connection points; see Connecting shapes with lines.
  • Show Rulers – shows or hides the horizontal/vertical rulers along the canvas edges.
